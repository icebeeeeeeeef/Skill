# Quality Rubric

Score community and engineering articles by evidence signals before relying on them.

## Strong Signals

- First-party author, maintainer, or named engineering team.
- Links to source code, PR, issue, paper, or official docs.
- Software versions, dates, hardware, workload, and environment are stated.
- Includes code, configs, logs, or reproducible commands.
- Contains benchmarks with methodology and limitations.
- Explains trade-offs, failed attempts, and boundary conditions.
- Part of a coherent series by the same author.
- Original publication, not a repost.

## Weak Signals

- No date or version.
- No links to original evidence.
- Rewrites a paper or README without additional verification.
- Contains broad claims such as "提高很多" without measurement.
- SEO-heavy title, duplicated paragraphs, or unrelated keyword stuffing.
- Uses screenshots only where code or links should exist.

## Minimum Use Rule

For architecture decisions or project direction, cite community content only after checking at least one first-party source for the same core claim.
